# Paper figure captions

Figure 2. Cell-macro errors for the thirteen estimators under two source-label schedules. Small colored circles show five seed results, diamonds their means, and open circles deterministic results. M05 is highlighted. Panel ranges differ by budget and metric; all errors are in pp.

Figure 3. Stride10 predictions at all 109 primary events in six development cells. Black markers show measured next-reference capacity, blue dashed lines Ridge, and orange traces all five M05 seeds. Capacity is normalized by 2 Ah; the horizontal axis is the input ordinary-cycle index. Connecting lines are visual guides.

Figure 4. Cell-level RMSE differences against M03 and M06. Positive values favor M05; colored symbols show all five seeds and diamonds their means. Both budgets use the same symmetric scale.

Figure 5. Budget interaction and cell-composition sensitivity. (a) Each seed averages six paired cell-level interactions. (b,c) RMSE differences after excluding one cell or one target pair from aggregation, averaged across five seeds. No model is refitted for these subsets.

Figure 6. Residual alignment, correction energy and amplitude diagnostics. (a,b) Each point is a cell-seed pair; points above the diagonal improve MSE at amplitude one. (c,d) Faint lines show five seeds and bold lines their means at three specified diagnostic amplitudes. The deployed amplitude is one.
